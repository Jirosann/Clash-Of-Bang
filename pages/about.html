The History of BaNG

Clash of BaNG originated as a small online forum discussing strategies and sharing fan art for the legendary Clash universe. Today, it stands as the most prestigious community spanning across Jakarta, Bandung, and Sanur.

2018 - The First Spark

The founders met through a chaotic Clan Wars League and decided to form a dedicated forum to share Dark Troop strategies.

2020 - The Bandung Expansion

As the community grew, the first regional hub was established in Bandung, creating an offline presence for physical tournaments.

2022 - Art & Lore Division

A special division was created for fan arts and lore building, quickly becoming the most popular section of our community.

Present - Global Reach

With hubs in Jakarta and Sanur fully operational, Clash of BaNG launches its official unified web platform.
